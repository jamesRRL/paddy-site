# Privacy Policy for Paddy

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

Paddy is a recovery companion built around a single promise: **your recovery data is yours, and it stays on your device.** This policy explains what that means in practice.

## The short version

- **No account, no sign-up.** Paddy never asks for your email, phone number, or any identity.
- **No data collection.** Paddy has no servers of its own and no analytics. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information — because we never receive it in the first place.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Your check-ins, recovery actions, slips, "My Why" notes and photos, and settings are stored in Paddy's private storage on your phone.
- **No tracking.** Paddy contains no advertising, no third-party trackers, and no analytics SDKs.

## What Paddy stores (on your device only)

All of the following is saved locally and is never sent anywhere by Paddy:

- Your check-ins (mood, HALT, craving intensity, and any optional notes)
- Recovery actions you log
- Slips you record, and any reflection notes you write
- "My Why" items — reasons, letters, and any photos you add
- Your settings — clean date, trusted contacts, people you've chosen to steer clear of (a label and an optional note — never a phone number), daily reminder time, crisis hotline number, an optional display name, and an optional recovery focus

You are always in control of this data. Deleting Paddy deletes it from your device.

## Device backups

Like most apps, Paddy's on-device data is included in your phone's own backups (for example iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), so it can be restored to a new phone. Those backups are controlled by you and your backup provider under their terms — Paddy never sees them. If you don't want Paddy's data in your backups, you can exclude Paddy in your phone's backup settings.

## Features that use your phone's own apps

Some features hand off to apps already on your phone. When they do, those apps — not Paddy — carry out the action, under their own terms and your mobile carrier's:

- **"Reach out"** opens your phone's Messages or Phone app to text or call a contact you chose. Paddy does not send the message itself and keeps no record of the conversation. Your carrier and the person you contact will see the message or call.
- **"Get help"** dials a crisis line (988 by default, or a number you set) or the SAMHSA National Helpline. The call connects through your carrier to that service; Paddy is not part of the call.
- **"Find a meeting" and "Find treatment"** open those public websites in an in-app browser. Those sites — not Paddy — handle anything you do there, under their own privacy policies.

## Photos, notifications, and other permissions

- **Photos** — Paddy uses the iPhone's system photo picker, so Paddy never has access to your photo library: it receives only the photo you choose. That photo is copied into Paddy's private storage on your device, together with any information the photo itself carries (for example, where it was taken). It is never uploaded.
- **Notifications** — only if you turn on the daily check-in reminder in Settings. The reminder is scheduled on your device; no notification content is sent anywhere, and the reminder text never mentions recovery, so it is safe on a lock screen.
- Paddy does **not** use the **camera**, **microphone**, **location**, or **contacts**. Trusted contacts are typed in by you.

## Children

Paddy concerns substance-use recovery and is intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and Paddy does not knowingly collect information from anyone — it does not collect personal information at all.

## A note on safety

Paddy is a self-help companion. It is **not medical advice and not a crisis service.** If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.
