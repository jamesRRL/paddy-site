# Paddy support

**If you are in danger right now, call 911.** For thoughts of suicide or a mental-health crisis, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). For substance use, the **SAMHSA National Helpline** is free, confidential, and open 24/7: **1-800-662-4357**. Paddy is a self-help companion, not a crisis service.

## Contact

Questions, problems, or ideas: **[support@thetraide.com](mailto:support@thetraide.com)**. We read every message. Please don't include anything about your recovery that you wouldn't want in an email — we never need it to help.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Paddy has no accounts, no sign-up, and no servers. Open it and start.

**Where is my data?**
Only on your phone, in Paddy's private storage. Nothing is uploaded. Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) includes it, so it moves with you to a new phone if you restore from a backup. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

**How do I delete everything?**
Delete the Paddy app. Because there is no server, that removes every copy Paddy has.

**"Get help now" called the wrong line.**
It calls 988 by default. Paddy is available in the United States; if you need a different number, set it in Settings → Crisis hotline.

**The daily reminder doesn't arrive.**
Turn it on in Paddy's Settings, then check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Paddy is allowed. The reminder text is deliberately discreet ("A moment for you") so it is safe on a lock screen.

**I logged a slip. Did I lose my progress?**
No. A slip changes one number — your current days. Your longest run, lifetime days, every action you logged, and your showing-up streak all stay.
